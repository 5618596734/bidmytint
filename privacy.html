Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

BidMyTint respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

When you use BidMyTint, we may collect information necessary to operate the marketplace and connect customers with window film professionals. This may include:

  • Name, email address, and telephone number.
  • Project address, city, ZIP code, and property information.
  • Window film project details, measurements, notes, and preferences.
  • Photos uploaded in connection with a project.
  • Contractor account, business, service area, and profile information.
  • Proposals, pricing, product information, warranties, and availability.
  • Project status, selections, confirmations, and transaction records.
  • Technical information associated with use of the website, such as browser, device, and basic usage information where available.

3. How We Use Information

We use information collected through BidMyTint to operate and improve the marketplace, process project submissions, allow qualified professionals to review available projects, facilitate proposals, communicate project updates, provide customer support, maintain platform security, process project-related transactions, and administer customer and contractor accounts.

4. Customer Contact Information

BidMyTint is designed to protect customer contact information during the proposal process. Customer contact information and the full project address are not intended to be openly displayed to contractors while they are competing for a project.

When the applicable project confirmation process has been completed, information necessary for the selected contractor and customer to communicate and proceed with the project may be made available.

5. Project Information and Photos

Project details, approximate measurements, photos, general location information, and other information necessary to prepare a proposal may be made available to eligible contractors through the BidMyTint platform.

Users should avoid including unnecessary personal, confidential, or sensitive information in photographs, project descriptions, or other uploads.

6. Contractors and Proposals

Contractors may provide BidMyTint with business and account information and may submit proposals containing pricing, products, warranty information, availability, and other project-related information. BidMyTint uses this information to operate the proposal and project selection process.

7. Payments

Certain payments made through BidMyTint may be processed by third-party payment processors such as Stripe. Payment processors may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

BidMyTint may receive and retain transaction information such as payment status, amount, transaction reference, project reference, and related records necessary to administer the platform.

8. Service Providers

BidMyTint may use third-party service providers for services such as website hosting, database infrastructure, authentication, email delivery, file storage, payment processing, security, and other technology necessary to operate the platform.

Information may be provided to these service providers when reasonably necessary for them to perform services on behalf of BidMyTint.

9. When Information May Be Disclosed

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to operate the BidMyTint service, complete a transaction requested by a user, protect the security or integrity of the platform, prevent fraud or misuse, comply with applicable law or legal process, or protect the rights and safety of BidMyTint, its users, or others.

10. Data Security

BidMyTint uses reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information maintained through the platform. However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate BidMyTint, maintain project and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal or accounting obligations, and protect the platform and its users.

12. Your Choices

Users may contact BidMyTint to request assistance regarding their personal information, account information, or privacy questions. Certain information may need to be retained when required for legitimate business, transaction, security, or legal purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

BidMyTint is intended for adults and businesses seeking or providing window film services. The platform is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BidMyTint may update this Privacy Policy as the platform, services, or legal requirements change. When the policy is updated, the effective date displayed at the top of this page may also be updated.

15. Contact BidMyTint

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how BidMyTint handles information, please visit the Customer Support section on the BidMyTint website.
