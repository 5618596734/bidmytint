SmartMeasure Test

Estimate window dimensions from a photo using one known-size reference.

For this first test:

1. Use ONE standard US letter-size sheet of paper (8.5 × 11 inches).
2. Place the sheet flat against an accessible window.
3. Make sure the entire sheet and the entire window glass are visible in the photo.
4. Take the picture as straight-on as reasonably possible.

Never climb or reach an unsafe window for this test.
